SIMPLE TMS · DESIGN

Screens

The first drawn screens for Simple TMS, a small open-source transport management system for businesses that deliver with their own trucks and also send freight with outside carriers. Like Simple WMS's Game Mode, the dispatcher runs the day from a toy-town map of the depot, the roads and each customer stop, and every job opens as a drawer over it. The driver app works like the WMS scanner: big buttons, one stop at a time, and a "Ding!" when a stop is delivered. Each screen is a static snapshot in its starting state.

All names, numbers, places and references are made-up samples. The colours are the first draft from the design doc: road green for buttons and your trucks, amber for due soon and waiting, red only when a person needs to look.
